📋 How to connect

  1. Make sure WorkSense AI is running on your PC
  2. On your PC, run: ipconfig and find your IPv4 address
  3. Enter the IP address above (e.g. 192.168.1.100)
  4. Both devices must be on the same WiFi network
  5. Start WorkSense with: --host 0.0.0.0 flag
